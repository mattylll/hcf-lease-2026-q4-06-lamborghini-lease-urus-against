Leasing a Lamborghini: Why the Urus and the Revuelto Are Separate Questions

Of the roughly 320 people a month who search in the UK for a Lamborghini lease, a large share are thinking about one car. Google Ads data for November 2026 shows about 260 monthly searches for a Urus lease on its own. That tells you something about how the marque has changed, and it shapes the leasing conversation more than anything else.

A lease can mean contract hire, where you rent the car and return it, or lease purchase, where you buy it with part of the price deferred. We arrange both, for companies and individuals. On a Lamborghini, the model decides which is realistic.

The Urus: a performance car that behaves like a company car

The Urus is a four door SUV with five seats and a usable boot. It is driven daily, in all weathers, by people who would never commute in a mid engined coupe. On our Lamborghini finance page the Urus Performante is listed from £200,000.

From a funder’s side, that usability matters. A car bought in larger numbers and used as everyday transport builds a used market with plenty of evidence in it. Evidence is what a contract hire funder needs in order to forecast a future value and commit to a rental. So the Urus is the Lamborghini on which contract hire is the most realistic request, for businesses in particular.

We do not publish a Urus rental, and would caution against any figure quoted without a term, mileage and specification. Funders’ views differ and they change. The honest approach is to gather live quotes on the exact car.

The questions a business should ask before hiring a Urus

Because a Urus is often run through a company, three points deserve attention.

VAT. HMRC’s VAT Notice 700/64 says a business leasing a qualifying car normally cannot recover 50% of the VAT on the rentals. The other half is reclaimable under the normal rules.

Deduction of hire costs. HMRC’s Business Income Manual restricts the deduction for the cost of hiring a car by 15% where its emissions exceed 50g/km. Check the emissions figure of the exact variant with your accountant.

Private use. If a director uses the car privately, GOV.UK is clear that company car tax applies to that individual, based on the car’s value and emissions.

None of that makes contract hire wrong. It means the rental is not the whole cost, and that a purchase agreement should be costed alongside it. We do not give tax advice, and these are points for your accountant.

Huracan Tecnica and its successors: the middle ground

The Huracan Tecnica is listed from £217,000. V10 Lamborghinis have a well established used market, but they are bought as second or third cars and their values respond sharply to mileage and specification. Contract hire may be available with a modest mileage allowance. Many buyers prefer lease purchase, because they would rather not have a contractual limit on how far they drive a car bought for pleasure.

Revuelto and the V12 line: a purchase structure

The Revuelto is listed from £450,000 and the Aventador Ultimae from £350,000. At this level we would not expect contract hire to be the practical route. The sums are large, production is limited, and the car’s future value depends on factors no funder can model with confidence.

That is not a disadvantage for the buyer. Flagship V12 cars are the ones people keep, and a purchase structure leaves any strength in the car’s value with the owner. Lease purchase is the usual choice, with hire purchase for those who want no final payment at all.

Worked figures at both ends of the range

Across our lender panel the indicative band on current cars is 8.9 to 9.9 per cent. Lease purchase deposits on high value cars typically run from 20 to 35 per cent and terms from 24 to 60 months.

Urus Performante at £200,000. A deposit of £40,000 leaves £160,000 to fund. With an agreed final payment of £80,000 over 48 months at 9.9 per cent, the monthly payment is about £2,685. On hire purchase with no final payment it would be about £4,050.

Revuelto at £450,000. A deposit of £135,000 leaves £315,000 to fund. With an agreed final payment of £200,000 over 36 months at 9.9 per cent, the monthly payment is about £5,355.

Both final payments are round figures chosen to show the sum. They are not forecasts of value, and we do not publish forecasts.

Personal agreements

Individuals can take either route through us, and there is no minimum advance. Where an agreement is regulated, it is arranged through an FCA authorised broker partner. On personal contract hire, note that the right under the Consumer Credit Act 1974 to end a regulated hire agreement after 18 months does not apply where payments exceed £1,500 a year, so the contract’s own termination terms are what count.

Handing a Urus back

If you do hire, plan for the end at the start. Large wheels are easily kerbed, and performance tyres and carbon ceramic brakes are expensive consumables. Check whether a maintained agreement covers them. Photograph the car on delivery. Have any damage repaired by a specialist before collection.

The manufacturer’s own finance

Lamborghini has its own financial services offering through its dealer network, and it is the obvious comparison on a new car. We are independent of it and of every manufacturer. Brokers earn their place on used and privately sold cars and where several funders need comparing.
